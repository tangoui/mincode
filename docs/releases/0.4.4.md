**新增与改进**

- AI 遇到需求不明确或有几种合理做法时，会在时间线中直接向你提问。可以单选、多选、对比选项预览，选择"其他"自己填写或添加备注，也可以跳过。回答后折叠成一行，展开可查看问题和你的回答。
- 本地 Agent（Claude Code、Codex）的提问和表单也在时间线中回答，答案直接传回 Agent。Agent 撤回的提问会留下一行记录。

---

**New and improved**

- When a request is ambiguous or has several reasonable approaches, the AI asks you directly in the timeline. Pick one or several options, compare option previews, write your own answer under "Other", add a note, or skip. Answered questions collapse to one line that you can expand to review the questions and your answers.
- Questions and forms from local agents (Claude Code, Codex) are answered in the timeline too, and your answers go straight back to the agent. Questions an agent withdraws leave a short record.
