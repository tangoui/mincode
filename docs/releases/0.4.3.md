**新增与改进**

- AI 会话把消息、工具调用、思考、计划和审批排在同一条时间线上，并合并连续的只读操作。子会话回报紧跟对应的派发调用，派发入口移到输入框的附件按钮旁。
- 长工具输出可原地展开。命令输出默认显示末尾，其他长内容默认显示开头。截断的 JSON 仍按代码显示，成功和失败的工具调用用不同颜色标识。
- 吸顶提示词给时间线控件留出空间。会话轮次末尾的消息元数据在悬停或聚焦时显示，活动栏在有隐藏项目的一侧渐隐。

---

**New and improved**

- AI conversations now put messages, tool calls, reasoning, plans, and approvals on one timeline and group consecutive read-only actions. Child conversation reports appear below the corresponding delegation call, and the delegation button sits next to attachments.
- Expand long tool output in place. Command output shows its end by default, while other long content shows its beginning. Truncated JSON still appears as code, and successful and failed tool calls use distinct icon colors.
- Pinned prompts leave room for timeline controls. End-of-turn message metadata appears on hover or focus, and activity bar edges fade where items are out of view.
